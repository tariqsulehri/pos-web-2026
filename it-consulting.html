Our Methodology

From Concept to Live Implementation

Our three-phase consulting methodology takes your technology initiative from early-stage thinking all the way through to a fully deployed, supported solution.

💡
Phase 1

Concept to Technology

We begin by assessing the viability of digitisation for your specific context and selecting the optimal technology stack. This phase answers the critical question: what technology should power your vision, and why?

  • → Digitisation viability assessment
  • → Technology stack selection
  • → Programming language evaluation
  • → Platform recommendations
🗺️
Phase 2

Technology to Project Plan

With the right technology selected, we conduct a thorough business analysis and build a concrete implementation roadmap. This phase turns strategic intent into an actionable, time-bounded plan.

  • → Business requirements analysis
  • → Software selection (CRM, ERP, DMS, CMS)
  • → Development scope & customisation plan
  • → Short & long-term software roadmap
🚀
Phase 3

Project Plan to Implementation

The plan becomes reality. We design and build your custom solution, manage the deployment, and provide post-launch support to ensure a smooth, successful transition into production.

  • → Custom software design & build
  • → Deployment & installation
  • → Team training
  • → Post-deployment monitoring & support
