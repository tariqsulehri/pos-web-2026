Our Story

How It All Began in 1992

In the digital frontier of the early 1990s, where imagination intertwined with innovation, Point of Solutions emerged as a force to be reckoned with. Our story starts with a young programmer who created billing software that would change the way businesses operated.

When the system initially failed to produce printouts, the programmer investigated and discovered not a software flaw — but a disconnected network wire. This pivotal moment defined our holistic, hands-on problem-solving philosophy: always look beyond the obvious, always go deeper.

Born in 1992, we embarked on a journey fueled by passion and driven by a singular purpose — to sculpt the future through lines of code and the limitless possibilities of technology. You construct your business with foundational elements, and we refine it with a touch of technological brilliance.

Today, POS is a complete business solution trusted by more than 2,000 clients across 5+ countries, with a joint venture in agriculture and a growing footprint in enterprise technology.
