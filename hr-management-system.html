The Problem We Solve

HR Managers Deserve Better Than Paperwork

HR managers can become overwhelmed with paper-based tasks and the juggling of multiple disconnected applications — presenting a high risk of errors in payroll, attendance records, and employee data.

Manual processes don't just slow your HR team down — they create compliance risks, payroll mistakes, and frustrated employees who can't easily access their own records or request leave without chasing paper forms.

Our HRMS solves this at the root. It provides integrated HR applications accessible to both your HR department and every employee — automating routine functions like time-off balances, payroll deductions, and tax calculations through perfectly synchronised modules.

System Features

Every HR Function, Automated

From recruitment through to payslip — our HRMS handles the full employee lifecycle in one integrated platform.

👥
Employee Management
Complete employee profiles — personal info, documents, contracts, job history, and emergency contacts — all in one searchable database.
💰
Payroll Processing
Automated monthly payroll calculation with full salary components — basic pay, allowances, bonuses, and deductions — generating accurate payslips instantly.
✂️
Deduction Management
Manage EOBI, income tax, loans, and any custom deductions — all calculated automatically and reflected in every payslip.
🕐
Attendance Tracking
Real-time attendance recording with biometric integration support — track tardiness, overtime, and shift compliance automatically.
📅
Time-Off & Leave Management
Employee self-service for leave requests — automated balance tracking, approval workflows, and calendar visibility for managers.
📊
Performance Monitoring
Set performance targets, track KPIs, and record appraisal outcomes — building an objective, documented performance history for every employee.
🗄️
Real-Time Information Storage
All employee data stored securely in the cloud — accessible in real-time by authorised HR staff from any device, anywhere.
🧾
Automated Tax Calculations
Income tax and withholding tax calculated automatically per employee based on current Pakistani tax slabs — zero manual calculation.
🎓
Recruitment Management
Track job openings, manage applicants, record interviews, and onboard new hires — all within the same platform as your active workforce.
