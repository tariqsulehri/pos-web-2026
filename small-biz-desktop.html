Why Small Biz Desktop

Big-Business Technology at Small-Business Prices

Not every business needs a cloud subscription or enterprise ERP. Small and medium businesses need something reliable, fast, and affordable that gets out of the way and lets them focus on serving customers.

Small Biz Desktop is exactly that. It runs directly on your Windows PC — no internet dependency, no monthly fees, no complex setup. Your data stays on your machine, and your team is up and running in hours, not weeks.

It includes a full Accounts module, a complete Point of Sale system, and a built-in Restaurant system — three powerful tools bundled into a single, affordable package designed specifically for small and medium businesses in Pakistan.
